Stargazing

Receiving a telescope the christmas of 2020, I wasn't expecting to get so much use out of it. But boy was I wrong. Since that christmas, I have been stargazing at various planets and constellations using the "Travel Scope 70 Portable Telescope" from Celestron. While it isn't exactly the best telescope out in the market, you definitely get bang for your buck. As it's name, it is easy to packup in the backpack they provide to you and is a must-have when heading out into the country-side or any areas that have little light pollution. The only downside is it's phone clamp that is supposed to let you take photos, but in my experience, good luck. Regardless and besides the sudden telescope review, stargazing is an underated hobby to have and one I highly recommend.
